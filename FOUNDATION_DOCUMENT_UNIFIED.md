### UNIFIED WORLD BIBLE

#### PHYSICAL SETTING: 5th Century Romano-British Context
**Time Period:** c. 480-520 CE (Late 5th/Early 6th Century)
**Political Situation:** Post-Roman withdrawal, Saxon invasions, civilizational collapse
**Technology Level:** Advanced metalworking, Roman engineering knowledge, but declining infrastructure
**Social Structure:** Fragmenting from Roman administrative to emerging feudal patterns

**Key Environmental Elements:**
- **Sensory Palette:** Wood smoke, horse leather, weathering stone, morning mist
- **Daily Life:** Subsistence economy, seasonal isolation, tribal loyalties
- **Challenges:** Saxon raids, internal fragmentation, infrastructure decay, plague

#### MAGIC SYSTEM: HPMOR-Compatible Wounded Force
**Core Concept:** Magic is a living force wounded by the Atlantean catastrophe
**Current State:** Cautious, protective, harder to access than before
**The Interdict:** Natural scar tissue, not artificial spell
**Merlin's Role:** Could heal the wound, chooses restriction for safety

**Magic Limitations:**
- **Public use forbidden:** Creates dangerous dependencies
- **Written transmission blocked:** Interdict prevents systematic documentation
- **Healing carefully rationed:** Avoids pattern-matching to catastrophe conditions
- **Combat magic avoided:** Prevents magical arms race escalation

**Integration with Character Development:**
- **Arthur's arc:** Forces reliance on rational solutions, creates mystery to investigate
- **Merlin's arc:** Justifies protective secrecy, provides ongoing moral weight
- **Relationship arc:** Enables burden-sharing through demonstrated trustworthiness

#### THE MIRROR OF ERISED: Convergence Point
**Physical Description:** Ancient artifact, Atlantean script, hidden in Welsh barrow
**Function:** Shows Coherent Extrapolated Volition (what you would want if wiser)
**Arthur's Vision:** Himself choosing restriction and secrecy alongside Merlin
**Merlin's Vision:** Making the Interdict permanent, no longer alone in the choice

**Role in Story:**
- **Plot convergence:** Where both character arcs meet
- **Thematic crystallization:** Shows that wisdom sometimes means accepting limitation
- **Relationship catalyst:** Reveals compatibility of deepest values
- **Knowledge transfer:** Enables partial revelation without full exposition

**Acceptable:**
- Latin phrases for educated/formal speech

- Military/agricultural terminology
- Roman historical references

**Forbidden:**
- Modern slang or colloquialisms
- Psychological/scientific terminology
- Democratic political concepts
- Anachronistic technology references

**Chapter Structure:**
- **Arthur chapters:** Grounded governance focus, rational problem-solving

- **Integration points:** Shared scenes, complementary information reveal

- **Arthur knows:** Patterns in Merlin's behavior, practical governance challenges

- **Readers learn:** Through both perspectives, with dramatic irony carefully managed

**Tension Maintenance:**
- **Arthur's frustration:** Rational mind confronting inexplicable limitations

- **Resolution:** Shared burden without compromised mission
