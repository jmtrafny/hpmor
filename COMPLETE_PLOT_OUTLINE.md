### CHAPTER 2: The Healer's Secret
**POV:** Arthur | **Length:** 2,200 words

**Function:** First concrete evidence that Merlin has real power
**Plot Events:**
- A plague victim near death is mysteriously healed overnight
- Arthur investigates: no known healer visited, no treatments applied
- Witnesses describe an old man who "spoke words" over the child
- Arthur confronts Merlin: "You can heal. Why won't you heal everyone?"
- Merlin's response is true but incomplete: dangerous dependencies, unintended consequences

**Character Development:**
- Arthur's rationalist worldview shaken by inexplicable evidence
- First crack in his "charlatan" theory about Merlin
- Introduction of Arthur's core ethical framework: if you can help, you should help

**Information Revealed:**
- Merlin has genuine magical abilities

- Something makes public magical healing dangerous

### CHAPTER 4: The Pattern
**POV:** Arthur | **Length:** 2,300 words

**Function:** Arthur begins tracking Merlin's behavior systematically
**Plot Events:**
- Arthur documents every instance of Merlin's "refusals"
- Pattern emerges: never public, never the same method twice, never "too miraculous"
- Arthur realizes Merlin is following deliberate rules
- Interview with the healed child's mother: she knows not to speak of it, doesn't know why
- Arthur's hypothesis: Merlin is preventing something specific

**Investigation Elements:**
- Multiple healings documented but never witnessed directly
- Merlin's absences coincide with "miraculous" recoveries
- Magic-users in the kingdom are also dying disproportionately from plague
- Evidence that someone is deliberately maintaining ignorance

**Character Development:**
- Arthur applies rationalist investigation to magical mystery
- Growing respect for Merlin's competence, confusion about his limitations
- First hint that secrets might serve a purpose
