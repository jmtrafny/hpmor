# The Weight of Atlantis

**A dual-protagonist HPMOR-adjacent novella**

---

## Story Summary

In the twilight of Roman Britain, a 14-year-old peasant boy pulls a sword from a stone and becomes king. Arthur Pendragon believes in reason, in systems, in the radical idea that knowledge should be free and justice should be evidence-based.

At his court stands Merlin—an ancient wizard who has watched civilizations rise and fall. Merlin carries a terrible secret: the last civilization that democratized magic was erased so completely from existence that even memory of it vanished. The Benefactor who tried to share magical power with everyone was *right*—and still destroyed everything.

This is the story of two people learning to bear weight together. Of a rationalist king who must accept that some things cannot be explained. Of an exhausted guardian who finally finds someone to share his burden. Of the choice between justice and survival, and how restriction can be an act of mercy rather than tyranny.

## Key Characters

**Arthur Pendragon** (14-15)
- Peasant-born rationalist king
- Arc: Idealist → Keeper of Secrets
- Voice: Direct, logical, occasionally defensive

**Merlin** (500+ years)
- Ancient wizard bearing Atlantis's secret
- Arc: Exhausted Guardian → Shared Burden-Bearer
- Voice: Sparse, weighted, silence as eloquent as speech

**Sir Lancelot** (17-18)
- Enemies-to-friends with Arthur
- Arthur's closest confidant (but not told the deepest secret)
