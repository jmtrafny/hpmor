### Potential Continuity Risks

**RISK: Time of year inconsistency**
- Spring = plague arrives (ships thaw)
- Summer = heat, plague peaks, dragon possible
- Fall = harvest disrupted, plague subsiding
- Winter = cold, hunger, Round Table proposal brewing
- Spring (Year 2) = proposal crisis
- Summer (Year 2) = Mirror scene
- **Mitigation**: Track season explicitly in chapter headers during writing

**RISK: Arthur's physical location scene-to-scene**
- Fortress = most scenes
- Villages = some investigation scenes
- Barrow = Mirror scene (1 mile away)
- Northern territories = dragon battle
- Borderlands = Saxon conflicts
- **Mitigation**: Note location at top of each scene; track travel time

**RISK: What each character knows at each moment**
- Arthur's investigation must be gradual
- Can't know things he hasn't learned yet
- Merlin can't reveal information prematurely
- **Mitigation**: Use Knowledge Matrix (Section 5) before writing each scene

**RISK: Merlin's refusals must be consistent**
- NEVER helps openly
- ONLY helps secretly (selective healing, Saxon wizard death)
- Never uses combat magic in sight of others
- Never writes down powerful knowledge
- **Mitigation**: Before any Merlin scene, check: would this violate his principles?

**RISK: Magic manifestation inconsistency**
- Very low visibility (pre-institutional era)
- No formal spells shown (Interdict prevents written teaching)
- Manifestations should be: rare, unexplained, fearsome
- **Mitigation**: Magic appears 3-5 times total in story; each must be significant

**RISK: Historical anachronisms**
- Avoid: modern idioms, technology, social concepts
- Include: physical hardship, superstition, limited information, seasonal rhythms
- **Mitigation**: Review World Bible "Historical Authenticity Notes" before drafting

**RISK: Voice slippage**
- Arthur: brilliant but 14-15, idealistic, emotional, learns hard lessons
- Merlin: spare speech, long silences, weighted words, exhausted
- **Mitigation**: Read Character Profiles voice sections before drafting dialogue

**RISK: Mirror scene underwhelms**
- This is THE climax
- Must be worth the buildup
- Existing draft (MIDPOINT_CRUX.md) is strong
- **Mitigation**: All of Act 1-2 must build toward this; everything after must justify weight

## 10. CRITICAL SCENE DEPENDENCIES

### The Mirror Scene (MIDPOINT_CRUX.md) - What Must Happen Before

**For Arthur**:
1. Must have investigated Merlin's pattern of refusals ✓
2. Must have faced plague crisis where Merlin refused to help broadly ✓
3. Must have killed the systematization proposal by authority (his darkest moment) ✓
4. Must be desperate enough to accept Merlin's invitation to the barrow
5. Must trust Merlin *just enough* to come alone

**For Merlin**:
1. Must have tested Arthur thoroughly
2. Must have witnessed Arthur make the right choice (kill proposal) despite not knowing why
3. Must have decided Arthur is ready (or as ready as anyone can be)
4. Must be desperate enough to break 500 years of silence
5. Must have hope that Arthur can share the burden

**For Reader**:
1. Must care deeply about both characters ✓
2. Must be frustrated by Merlin's refusals (mirroring Arthur's frustration)
3. Must understand Arthur's principles (freedom of knowledge)
4. Must see the proposal as genuinely good (so Arthur's choice is painful)
5. Must be ready for revelation (enough mystery, enough escalation)

**Setup Checklist (Required Before Mirror Scene)**:
- [ ] Child Merlin heals (selective healing established)
- [ ] Multiple refusals shown (pattern established)
- [ ] Arthur observes pattern, tries to understand
- [ ] Plague crisis where refusal costs lives
- [ ] Saxon wizard encounter (Merlin refuses to fight magically)
- [ ] Saxon wizard found dead by magic (Merlin's secret action)
- [ ] Round Table flourishing (success creates danger)
- [ ] Young Scholar introduced and bonded with Arthur
- [ ] Proposal made (Arthur initially attracted to idea)

- [ ] Private confrontation: "civilizations that do this die"
- [ ] Arthur's impossible choice: kill proposal or risk everything
- [ ] Round Table fractures, Arthur feels like failure
- [ ] Merlin recognizes himself in Arthur's pain
- [ ] Merlin's decision: show Arthur the Mirror

### The Epilogue - What Must Be Shown

**Requirements**:
1. ~20 years have passed (Arthur ~34-36, Merlin unchanged)
2. Arthur has kept the secret without resentment
3. Merlin is no longer alone
4. Separation between magical and non-magical deepening

## 11. WRITER'S DAILY CHECKLIST

🚩 **Arthur knows something he hasn't learned yet**
