# The Weight of Atlantis: Multi-Agent Workflow Design
## A Coordinated Writing System for the Arthur/Merlin Novella

*Designed for the HPMOR-adjacent historical rationalist fiction project*

---

## Project Overview

### The Story
**Working Title:** *The Weight of Atlantis*

A dual-protagonist novella set in post-Roman Britain, exploring the origins of wizard-Muggle separation through:
- **Arthur:** A rationalist king trying to hold civilization together
- **Merlin:** A wizard carrying the terrible knowledge of Atlantis's destruction

**Target Length:** 55,000-70,000 words (~45 pieces: chapters + interludes)

**Existing Materials:**
- `ARTHUR_STORY_BIBLE.md` - Arthur's character and plot arc
- `MERLIN_STORY_BIBLE.md` - Cosmological background and Merlin's arc
- `COMBINED.md` - Unified story structure analysis
- `THE_MIRROR_SHOWS_ARTHUR.md` - Key scene development with draft prose
- `MIDPOINT_CRUX.md` - Mirror revelation scene (draft)
- `CHAPTER_ONE.md` - Opening chapter (complete draft)

## Recommended Workflow Pattern: **Hybrid with Dual-POV Adaptation**

### Why Hybrid?
This project has unique characteristics that favor the hybrid pattern:

1. **Existing Foundation** - Story bibles are substantially complete
2. **Dual Protagonist** - Arthur and Merlin POVs can be developed in parallel streams
3. **Complex Cosmology** - Requires careful continuity tracking
4. **Existing Drafts** - Some chapters are already written, requiring integration
5. **Literary Quality Goals** - Voice consistency is critical

### Phase 1: Foundation Synthesis (Parallel)

**Purpose:** Create complete character profiles and world bible building on existing materials.

**Parallel Agents:**

#### Character Designer
**Focus Areas:**
- **Arthur Voice Profile:** Rationalist king, modern thinking in period setting
- **Merlin Voice Profile:** Ancient wisdom, spare careful speech
- **Supporting Cast:** Ector, Bedivere, Brother Marcus, the young scholar
- **Relationship Dynamics:** Arthur-Merlin trust arc

**Special Instructions:**
- Reference existing voice work in `THE_MIRROR_SHOWS_ARTHUR.md`
- Distinguish Arthur's grounded POV from Merlin's cosmic POV
- Create voice comparison chart for dialogue consistency

#### World Builder
**Focus Areas:**
- **5th Century Britain:** Post-Roman collapse, physical environment
- **Magic Cosmology:** Integrate from `MERLIN_STORY_BIBLE.md`
  - Magic as living force
  - The wound/scar tissue concept
  - The Interdict's nature
- **The Mirror:** Physical description, behavior rules
- **Atlantis Fragments:** Sensory palette for vignettes
- **Daily Life:** What Arthur's court looks like, sounds like, smells like

**Special Instructions:**
- Reference `world-guide/` for HPMOR consistency
- Focus on sensory details for prose
- Document what magic CAN'T do (limitations)

**Post-Parallel: Creative Synthesizer**
- Merge character and world outputs
- Ensure characters fit their world
- Resolve any conflicts
- Create unified Foundation Document

**Human Gate Questions:**
- Do Arthur and Merlin's voices feel distinct?
- Does the world feel lived-in and real?
- Any missing elements for the story you want to tell?

### Phase 2: Structure (Sequential)

**Purpose:** Create complete chapter-by-chapter plot outline.

**Agent:** Plot Developer

**Inputs:**
- Unified Story Architecture
- Character Profiles
- World Bible
- Existing plot structure from `COMBINED.md`
- Existing draft chapters

**Special Structure Requirements:**

This novella uses a **braided narrative** structure:

| Part | Arthur Chapters | Merlin Pieces | Interludes |
|------|----------------|---------------|------------|
| Part 1: Surface | 8-10 chapters | 3-4 short interludes | 2-3 |
| Part 2: Depths | 5-6 chapters | 6-8 substantial chapters | 2-3 |
| Part 3: Sharing | 4-5 chapters | 4-5 chapters | 1-2 |

**Chapter Types to Plan:**
1. **Arthur Main Chapters:** Grounded governance, plague response, military decisions
2. **Merlin Interludes (Early):** Short, fragmentary, weight-carrying
3. **Merlin Full Chapters (Later):** Full POV, Mirror visions, Atlantis revelation
4. **Vignettes:** Deep past (Atlantis) / Deep future (Harry frame?)
5. **Integration Chapters:** Both characters together

**Existing Content to Integrate:**
- Chapter 1: The Sword and the Stone (exists - opening Arthur POV)
- Mirror Scene: THE_MIRROR_SHOWS_ARTHUR (exists - climactic scene)
- MIDPOINT_CRUX scene draft (exists - needs integration)

**Deliverables:**

**Human Gate Questions:**
- Does the pacing work for both character arcs?
- Are revelation moments properly placed?

- How do existing drafts fit?

### Phase 3: Writing (Hybrid)

**Purpose:** Draft the complete novella.

**Special Consideration: Dual-POV Parallelization**

This story's dual-protagonist structure enables a unique parallel approach:

```
ARTHUR STREAM                    MERLIN STREAM
─────────────────               ─────────────────
Ch.1 (exists)
                                Interlude 1
Ch.2
Ch.3 (Plague begins)           Interlude 2 (Mirror memory)
Ch.4 (Plague response)         Interlude 3 (Atlantis fragment)
Ch.5 (Discovery)
                    ↓ CONVERGENCE: Confrontation about healing ↓
Ch.6 (Investigation)           Full Ch: Benefactor's story
Ch.7 (Saxon wizard)            Full Ch: What Merlin sees
                    ↓ CONVERGENCE: Mirror scene ↓
                    [Integrated chapters from here]
```

**Writing Sub-Phases:**

#### 3A: Opening (Sequential)
**Why Sequential:** Establishes voice, tone, and the foundation everything builds on.

**Execution:**
1. **Scene Writer:** Review/polish Chapter 1 (exists)
2. **Scene Writer:** Draft Merlin Interlude 1
3. **Scene Writer:** Draft Chapter 2 (Arthur)
4. **Dialogue Specialist:** Voice calibration pass on all three pieces

**Calibration Focus:**
- Arthur's rationalist internal voice
- Merlin's spare, weighted speech
- The tonal contrast between POVs
- Period-appropriate language that feels modern in thought

**Human Gate:** Approve opening; confirm voices are correct before scaling

#### 3B: Middle (Parallel POV Streams)
**Why Parallel:** Arthur and Merlin chapters have minimal direct dependencies in the middle section.

**Stream A: Arthur Chapters**
- Scene Writer assigned to Arthur POV
- Chapters 3-7 (Plague arc, Saxon wizard)
- Uses Arthur voice profile
- Maintains grounded, governance focus

**Stream B: Merlin Pieces**
- Scene Writer assigned to Merlin POV
- Interludes 2-4, then full chapters
- Uses Merlin voice profile
- Carries cosmic weight, fragmentary early, fuller later

**Coordination Points:**
- Timeline must align
- Events Arthur witnesses, Merlin explains
- Knowledge consistency critical

**Chapter Coordinator** manages:
- Batch preparation (context packages)
- Cross-stream continuity
- Integration after each batch

**Human Gates:**
- After Plague arc complete
- After Saxon wizard arc complete
- At Midpoint (before convergence)

#### 3C: Climax (Sequential)
**Why Sequential:** Convergence requires careful threading; climactic scenes build directly on each other.

**Execution:**
1. Integrate existing Mirror scene draft (`MIDPOINT_CRUX.md`)
2. Write Arthur's Decision chapter
3. Write The Sharing sequence
4. Write Epilogue/Final Image
5. **Dialogue Specialist:** Polish all revelation dialogues

**Special Attention:**
- Arthur and Merlin together in the Mirror scene
- The partial telling—what Merlin reveals, what he holds back
- Arthur's choice to trust without full understanding
- The final image: Arthur speaking to knights, Merlin watching

**Human Gate:** Approve complete draft

### Phase 4: Polish (Parallel)

**Purpose:** Editorial review and consistency verification.

**Parallel Agents:**

#### Editor
**Focus Areas:**
- Structural editing: Does the dual-POV structure work?
- Pacing: Is the slow build appropriate for literary fiction?
- Line editing: Prose quality, voice consistency
- Special attention: Arthur's modern-thinking-in-period-setting voice
- Special attention: Merlin's transitions from spare to revealing

#### Continuity Keeper
**Critical Tracking:**
- Magic rules consistency with HPMOR
- Timeline of events across both POVs
- Character knowledge matrix (who knows what when)
- Physical continuity (injuries, locations, possessions)
- Interdict/scar tissue concept consistency
- What the Mirror shows vs. doesn't show

**Special Continuity Challenges:**
- Arthur's knowledge vs. Merlin's knowledge
- Reader knowledge vs. character knowledge
- Dramatic irony management
- HPMOR canon compatibility

**Post-Parallel: Creative Synthesizer**
- Merge editorial and continuity reports
- Prioritize revisions
- Execute revision pass
- Final consistency check

**Human Gate:** Final approval

## Special Considerations for This Project

### Voice Consistency
This project lives or dies on voice. Arthur and Merlin must sound different:

**Arthur:** Modern rationalist thinking expressed in period-appropriate language. Internal monologue is where the rationalism shows. Dialogue is practical, grounded.

**Merlin:** Spare, weighted, every word chosen. When he speaks at length (the revelations), it should feel like a dam breaking. The contrast between his silence and his speaking is the character.

### HPMOR Compatibility
Reference the `world-guide/` directory for:
- Magic system rules (`magic/interdict-of-merlin.md`, `magic/fundamental-rules.md`)
- Character references (Harry's future frame, if used)
- Cosmological consistency (`cosmology/source-of-magic.md`)

### Existing Draft Integration
The existing drafts are high quality but need integration:
- Chapter 1: Essentially complete, needs polish pass
- Mirror scene drafts: Multiple versions exist, need to choose/merge best elements
- Voice calibration should happen early to ensure new content matches

### Literary vs. Genre Expectations
This is literary historical fiction with genre elements (fantasy). This means:
- Slower pacing is acceptable
- Prose quality matters more than plot velocity
- Themes should be felt, not stated
- The "flash" concept: each scene needs a visceral central image
